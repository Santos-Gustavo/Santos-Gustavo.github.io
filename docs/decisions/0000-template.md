## Context

What problem, constraints and requirements justify this decision?

## Decision

What approach is proposed or authorized?

## Alternatives

What realistic alternatives were considered, and why were they not selected?

## Consequences

Describe benefits, complexity, trade-offs, operational costs and risks.

## Validation

How will the decision's important assumptions and quality attributes be verified?

## Approval

Record the designated decision-maker and approval status.

Use sequentially numbered ADRs for consequential new decisions. Do not create historical ADRs without evidence or claim that a proposed decision has been approved.
