## Billing — explicitly not next

Payments (Stripe, MB WAY, EuPago) are disabled/deferred, not scheduled. A positive MVP-VALIDATION-001 trial (3–5 contractors) leads to a **controlled beta with 5–10 contractors**, not billing work. Billing only starts after: at least 5 contractors use the app across 2+ real projects or 4+ real reports each, **and** at least 3 say they would pay or ask to keep using it. See `docs/validation/MVP-VALIDATION-001.md` §9–10.
